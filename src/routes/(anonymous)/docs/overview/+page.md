# Overview

DuitGee is a collaborative expense tracker for households. Couples, families with kids, multi-generational homes, flatmates with a joint pool — anywhere two or more people share money and need to stay on the same page about where it went. It works for solo users too: your "partner" just becomes future-you trying to remember what last Tuesday's charge was for.

## The two ideas behind every feature

**Reduce misunderstanding and friction.** The interpersonal kind, not the technical kind. The "did you log that yet?" loop, the bank notification nobody can immediately explain, the duplicate dinner two people enter from different phones, the "how much did we spend on dining out?" tax on every check-in.

**Spot gaps in spending behavior.** The patterns you can't see when you're in them. The installment that crept in. The dining-out drift from "treat" to "default." The forgotten subscription. The category that consistently runs over your mental model of normal.

Most features primarily serve one idea. Funds and recurring expenses serve both as enablers.

## What you'll find in DuitGee

- **Vaults** — a shared (or solo) container for one currency and one set of members. [Get started](/docs/getting-started).
- **Expenses** — the core record. One `paidBy`, one category, one fund, one set of tags. [Read more](/docs/expenses).
- **Funds** — descriptive wallets, not prescriptive budgets. [Read more](/docs/funds).
- **Recurring expenses** — schedule with auto or queue mode, with or without auto-generation. [Read more](/docs/recurring).
- **AI receipt scan** — drop a photo or PDF, get amount, merchant, date, and a suggested category. [Read more](/docs/ai-receipt-scan).
- **Statistics** — server-aggregated trends, breakdowns, and an AI reading of the period. [Read more](/docs/statistics).
- **Unidentified expenses** — the placeholder workflow for "I see the charge but don't know who spent it yet." [Read more](/docs/unidentified-expenses).

## What DuitGee is not

- Not a Splitwise replacement. No per-expense splits, no settle-up flow, no IOU ledger.
- Not a YNAB-style budget tool. Funds are descriptive wallets, not envelopes.
- Not a bank-syncing aggregator. Manual entry, AI scan, and CSV import are the data-in paths.
- Not multi-currency per expense. Currency lives at the vault level — use separate vaults per currency.

## Plan philosophy

Pro enhances. Free is fully usable. Free covers a real household's daily life — unlimited expenses, unlimited members, the entire unidentified workflow, one fund, five recurring rules, statistics for the last 12 months, attachments, and full CSV export. Pro adds scale, automation, depth, and AI. [Read more](/docs/plans-billing).
