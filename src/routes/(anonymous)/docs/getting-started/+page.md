# Getting Started

You can be set up in under five minutes.

## 1. Sign up

Go to **/register** and create an account with email + password. You can also start anonymously from the home page and link a real account later — anything you record while anonymous will move to the new account.

## 2. Create your first vault

A vault is the container for everyone and everything. One vault per shared pool of money in one currency.

1. Land on **/vaults** after signing in.
2. Click **New Vault**.
3. Pick a **name** ("Family", "Household", "Trip to Tokyo").
4. Pick a **locale** (formatting) and **currency**. Every expense in this vault will be in this currency — pick another vault for another currency.
5. Click **Create**.

Your new vault becomes the default — the one you land on when you sign in next time.

## 3. Invite the people who share money with you

The collaboration is the point. From the vault page, open **Members** and invite by email. They get a link, accept it, and start logging.

If you're going solo, skip this and continue. Every collaboration feature degrades gracefully to a single-user mode.

## 4. Log your first expense

From the vault home, hit **Add expense**:

- **Amount** — required.
- **Category** — pick from the seeded list (Groceries, Dining, Transport, Utilities, etc.).
- **Date** — defaults to now.
- **Paid by** — yourself, another member, or "Vault" for shared.
- **Payment type** — cash, debit, credit, transfer, e-wallet, QRIS, check, or other.
- **Note** — anything that helps later you.
- **Tags** — optional labels (the WHO/WHY dimension; see [Tags](/docs/tags)).
- **Fund** — optional pool to deduct from (see [Funds](/docs/funds)).

Hit save. You're done.

## 5. Set up the patterns

Once you've logged for a few days, plug in:

- A **fund** for a real wallet you care about (the household card, the kid's allowance, the trip pool).
- A **recurring rule** for rent, salary, the streaming subscription. See [Recurring expenses](/docs/recurring).
- A **template** for charges you log over and over — the same coffee, the same toll. See [Templates](/docs/templates).
